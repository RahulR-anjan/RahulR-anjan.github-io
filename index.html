Cloud Radiative Forcing & Aerosol–Climate Interactions

Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM), Pune — Forecasting Research Dept.

  • Built a multi-reanalysis intercomparison pipeline evaluating Cloud Radiative Forcing (CRF) from CERES EBAF against four reanalyses (ERA5, MERRA-2, JRA-55, NCEP/NCAR) over 2001–2023.
  • Designed a two-stage statistical attribution framework (OLS + Ridge fallback via statsmodels/scikit-learn) linking cloud properties and aerosol loading to CRF biases.
  • Developed a radiative kernel attribution pipeline using the RRTMG scheme to decompose CRF discrepancies into cloud fraction, condensate, and thermodynamic contributions.
  • Trained a PyTorch DNN emulator predicting six CERES CRF flux variables (GroupKFold CV, ENSO-onset-year holdout), achieving Test R² of 0.878–0.934.

Semester Project

Bioaerosol PM₁₀ Modelling & Health Risk Assessment

IISER Kolkata — Supervisor: Dr. Debananda Roy

  • Conducted active air sampling (liquid impinger, 2.0 L min⁻¹, 150 min) across four contrasting microenvironments with concurrent PM₁₀, temperature, RH, and wind speed monitoring.
  • Quantified culturable bacterial bioaerosol concentrations via dilution plating on LB agar and three selective media (MSA, MacConkey, Blood Agar); identified 6–8 colony morphotypes per site.
  • Found bacterial concentrations at two sites exceeded the WHO indoor benchmark by 56× and 8.7× despite lower indoor PM₁₀ mass — demonstrating PM mass is a poor proxy for biological inhalation risk.
  • Developed a hazard-quotient (HQ) bioaerosol risk model (HQ ≈ 39–60), extending aerosol health-risk methodology to a biological particulate context.
